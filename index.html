Beginner-friendly product strategy

Start with: House wires (0.75–4 sq mm), standard MCBs, 2×2 LED panels, PVC conduits — fast turnover (15–30 days), electrician-driven demand, minimal technical selling.

Avoid initially: VFDs, automation kits, custom industrial cables — require engineering knowledge, 90+ day turnover, and OEM authorizations.

Sweet spot: LED lighting (8–12% margins, low capital ₹3L) and modular switches (4–7% margins, brand-driven pull).

As a new entrant, use Parallel Procurement (sub-wholesaling from Tier-1 distributors in Indore/Delhi/Ahmedabad at 60–70% off MRP) instead of chasing direct brand distributorships. This preserves working capital and builds sales history for future authorization.

07 — Risk Mitigation

Operational Risk Framework

Wholesale electrical has three primary failure modes for first-timers. Each has a clear mitigation strategy.

Critical Risk

Credit Extension Trap

Traditional channels demand 30–90 day credit. New entrants who extend credit without collections infrastructure face cash flow collapse within 6 months.

✓ Mitigation: Cash/IMPS/RTGS only in Year 1. Position as "lower margin, zero credit" dealer.
High Risk

Inventory Obsolescence

Decorative lighting and modular switch aesthetics shift rapidly. Dead stock ties up 20–30% of working capital.

✓ Mitigation: Stock neutral project whites/grays only. Avoid high-end consumer-facing designs.
Medium Risk

Commodity Price Shocks

Copper/aluminum price swings on global exchanges can erase wire/cable margins overnight.

✓ Mitigation: Thin high-velocity wire stock. Pass price revisions same-day with Tier-1 partners.
